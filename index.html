Who we are

FantasyFitness is an iPhone app where friends compete in weekly head-to-head matchups based on their daily step counts. It is operated by Vatsal Rami, an individual developer ("we", "us").

Questions about this policy or your data: vatsalrami@gmail.com

What we collect

  • Your Apple account identifier. You sign in with Sign in with Apple. Apple gives us an identifier for your account in this app and, if you choose to share it, your real email address or Apple's private relay address. We never see your Apple password.
  • Your display name. The name you choose in the app.
  • Your daily step totals. With your permission, the app reads your step count from Apple Health and uploads one total per calendar day, together with the time of your last sync. We read steps only, and the access is read-only: we never write anything to Apple Health. Steps that were entered by hand in the Health app are not counted.
  • Your leagues and results. The leagues you create or join, your matchups, scores and standings.
  • Corrections to your step totals. A league commissioner can correct a day's steps. We record the day, the totals before and after, the reason the commissioner wrote, who made the correction and when.
  • Your consent. When you agreed to the health-data consent and which version of it.
  • Basic technical data. Our hosting provider automatically logs basic technical data about requests to the service, such as IP address and time.

The app also keeps a temporary copy of your recent step totals in its cache on your phone, so numbers appear quickly.

Why we use it

  • To run the weekly matchups, calculate scores and standings, and show them to your league.
  • To keep the game fair, for example through logged commissioner corrections.
  • To operate, secure and fix the service, and to answer you if you contact us.

Who can see your data

  • Other members of the leagues you join can see your display name, your daily step totals, and your matchups and scores in that league. This is how the game works: your league sees how many steps you took.
  • The league commissioner can correct a day's step total. Every correction is logged, with the reason and who made it, and is visible to the members of that league.
  • People who are not in your leagues cannot see your data in the app. Other players do not see your email address.
  • Us. As the operator, we can access the database to run and support the service.

We share data with the service providers listed below, and nobody else.

What we do not do

  • We do not show advertising, and the app contains no third-party advertising or analytics code.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your health data for marketing or data mining.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not store your health data in iCloud.

Service providers

  • Supabase provides our database, authentication and server functions. Our project is hosted in the US West (Oregon) region, so your data is stored and processed in the United States.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and Apple Health. Your Apple Health data stays on your device and under Apple's terms, except for the daily step totals the app uploads as described above.

How long we keep your data

We keep your data while your account exists. A league's weekly step history is kept for as long as your account exists. If our hosting provider keeps routine backups, deleted data can remain in them until they expire.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Me, then Delete account. Deleting your account is permanent. It erases your profile and your step history, and closes your sign-in, which removes your email address and Apple identifier from our authentication system.

What remains after you delete your account:

  • Your name is removed. In past league results you appear as "Deleted player".
  • Finalized weekly results of past matchups are kept, without your name or step details, so other players' history stays intact.
  • A minimal record that an account existed and when it was deleted, with the date and version of its consent. It contains no name, email or step data.
  • If you made corrections as a league commissioner, the log keeps them under "Deleted player".
  • If you were a league commissioner, the role passes to another member of the league. If no other member remains, the league is deleted.
  • If you are in a league whose season is under way, you stay in its schedule as "Deleted player" so the other players' matchups still work.

Your choices

You can stop sharing steps at any time: open Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Health, then FantasyFitness, and turn off access to steps. The app can then no longer see new steps. Steps already uploaded stay in your leagues' history until you delete your account.

Children

FantasyFitness is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Access to your data is restricted by account and league membership rules in the database, and data is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we publish a new version with a new effective date. When the change needs your consent, the app asks you again.
