Common questions

My steps are not showing. Open Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Health, then Lockstep, and turn on Steps. Then open Lockstep so it can sync. Manually entered steps in the Health app are not counted.

How do I join a league? Ask your commissioner for the invite code (it looks like FIT-ABCD), then tap "Join with a code" in the app.

How do I delete my account? In the app, open Settings, then Delete account, and follow the steps. Deleting your account is permanent. See the privacy policy for what is removed.

A step total looks wrong. Your league commissioner can correct a day's steps; every correction is logged and visible to the league.
